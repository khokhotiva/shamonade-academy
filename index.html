Shamonade Academy is a registered early childhood development centre dedicated to providing quality care and education for children aged 0–6 years.

We also offer aftercare and extra lessons for Grade R–7 learners, both in-person and online — helping every child build confidence, strengthen their skills and reach their full potential.
